2. Single Phase vs Three Phase
Residential = Single Phase
120/240V
Used for:
* Houses
* Apartments
* Small shops
Voltage:
* Hot → Neutral = 120V
* Hot → Hot = 240V
Used for:
* Lights
* Receptacles
* Dryers
* Water heaters
* AC condensers■

120/208V Wye
Common in:
* Offices
* Restaurants
* Retail
277/480V Wye

* Warehouses
* Industrial plants
* Large HVAC
* Commercial lighting

3. Panel Types You MUST Know
Main Service Panel
* First disconnect after meter
* Has main breaker
* Neutral + ground bonded together
■
Subpanel* Fed from main panel
* Neutral and ground MUST be separated
* Requires:
* 2 hots
* neutral
* ground
MOST COMMON APPRENTICE MISTAKE:
Bonding neutral and ground in subpanel.
DO NOT DO THIS.
■
Disconnect Switch
Used for:
* HVAC
* Motors
* Equipment servicing
Can shut off local equipment power.
■
MCC (Motor Control Center)
Industrial cabinet containing:
* Motor starters
* VFDs
* Breakers
* Overloads
■
Transformers
Used to step voltage:
* Up
* Down
Example:
480V → 208V transformer.

8. Grounding vs Bonding
Grounding
Connection to earth.
Purpose:
* Stabilize voltage* Lightning/surge protection
■
Bonding
Connecting metal parts together.
Purpose:
* Fault current path
* Trip breaker quickly
■
9. GFCI vs AFCI
GFCI
Protects PEOPLE from shock.
Trips around:
* 4–6mA leakage
Required:
* Bathrooms
* Kitchens
* Garages
* Outdoors
■
AFCI
Protects against ARC faults/fire.
Required in:
* Bedrooms
* Living areas

13. Lockout Tagout (LOTO)
Before Working:1. Shut off power
2. Lock disconnect/breaker
3. Tag with your name
4. VERIFY power is dead
Best phrase:
“Test before touch.”

21. MOST IMPORTANT SAFETY RULES
PPEWear:
* safety glasses
* gloves
* hearing protection
* proper boots
■
Arc Flash
480V can kill instantly.
Respect ALL gear.
■
Ladder Rule
Never stand on top cap.
■
Verify Meter First
Before checking voltage:
1. Test meter on known live source
2. Test circuit
3. Re-test live source
This proves meter works.
