### Power Flow Overview
Understanding how AC power travels through the Musco LED system is essential for identifying potential faults:

1. **Power Input:** AC power enters the system through the disconnect switch located in the bottom right corner of the enclosure. The top of the disconnect is the line side, while the bottom is the load side. Rotating the disconnect 90 degrees counterclockwise turns the system off, and rotating it 90 degrees clockwise turns it on.
2. **Distribution Blocks:** Power flows from the disconnect into the distribution blocks. Depending on the site configuration, this may be an A, B, and C block set, an A and B block (phase-to-phase), or may include a neutral block (phase-to-neutral).
3. **Fuse Protection:** Power travels to the line side (left side) of the fuse holders and exits through the load side (right side).
4. **Driver Input:** Power moves from the fuse holders into the driver via the primary harness. 

### Driver Components
Each driver features three specific harnesses:
* **Power In/Primary Harness:** Contains a black wire, a green wire, and a blue wire with a white stripe.
* **Power Out/Secondary Harness:** Contains a red wire and a black wire.
* **Dimming Harness:** Plugs into the face of the driver.

### Controller and Output
Final power distribution depends on whether a communication controller is installed:
* **With Communication Controller:** Power flows from the driver to the controller, through the driver input on the controller, and out the luminaire output to the pole harness.
* **Without Communication Controller:** If no communication controller or high/medium/low dimming board is present, the driver output connects directly to the pole harness.
